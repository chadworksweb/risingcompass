What counts as a finding

A charge that does not match the lyric. A tier resting on the wrong side of a line. A methodology claim that bends under a real case. A number with nothing behind it. Aim at one song or at the framework itself.

How intake runs

  1. Apply at this desk.
  2. We read every application ourselves.
  3. Approved auditors file findings against a song or the framework.
  4. We re-examine each one and correct the record in the open.

No score, no rank, no payout. This is stewardship of the instrument, not a contest. What you get back is a Compass that holds up, and corrections that carry your name where they land.
