What is The Rising Compass?

The Rising Compass is a consciousness diagnostic tool for popular music. It measures the aggregate moral and vibrational charge of the songs that dominate popular culture — the ones heard most, shared most, and internalized most.

The Five Charge Levels

AscendedMusic that lifts consciousness ElevatedPositive, authentic expression BalancedNeither elevating nor degrading DegradedMaterialistic, shallow messaging CorruptedActively harmful content

How the Compass Works

Each song in the top 10 is evaluated and assigned a charge color. The compass position is a weighted average — songs ranked higher on the chart carry more weight, because they reach more people. Position #1 has 10x the weight of position #10.

Contamination

Contamination tracks songs in the top 3 tiers (Ascended, Elevated, Balanced) that carry content undercutting or distracting from being 100% pure. A song can score well overall but still have elements that compromise its integrity. Contamination doesn't change the compass — it's a separate signal about purity and quick-hitting injections/subliminal notions.

Data Source

Historical data comes from an analysis of 650+ Billboard Hot 100 #1 hits spanning 1960-2024, originally published on chadrising.com. Daily readings use current chart data from major streaming platforms.
