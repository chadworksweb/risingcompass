RSS Feeds

The daily readings, delivered to whatever you already read.

Each chart has its own feed. A new entry arrives once that day's reading is published, carrying the charge for the day, the editorial written with it, and the full ranked list with every song's tier and score. Paste a URL below into your reader.

These feeds are in beta. If something arrives broken, reads wrong in your reader, or is missing entirely, tell us through the general inquiry form.

  • Spotify (US) Spotify Top 50, daily https://risingcompass.net/feeds/spotify.xml
  • iTunes iTunes Downloads, daily https://risingcompass.net/feeds/itunes.xml
  • Shazam Shazam Top 200, daily https://risingcompass.net/feeds/shazam.xml
  • YouTube YouTube Trending, daily https://risingcompass.net/feeds/youtube.xml
  • New Music Friday New releases, every Friday https://risingcompass.net/feeds/new-music-friday.xml

What lands in your reader

One entry per chart day, titled with the date and that day's charge. Open it and you get the editorial for the reading, the degree, and every charting song with its tier, its score, and a link straight to its page here. Songs waiting on lyrics sit out until they are read.

Using them elsewhere

These are plain RSS 2.0, so anything that speaks the format can take them: a reader, an automation tool, a newsletter service, a bot. The readings are already public on the chart pages, and the feeds carry the same thing in a form other software can pick up.

Feeds update within about fifteen minutes of a reading being published. Entries stay put once they appear, so a reader will not resurface a day you have already seen. The last thirty chart days are available in each feed; everything before that lives in the calendar.
