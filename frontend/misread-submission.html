Report a Misread

Music is personal, so we understand that a classification can feel wrong when it's a song you love. This is why the Rising Compass measures lyrical content against a fixed rubric, not taste, talent, or personal interpretation.

If you believe we misread the content: missed context, missed context from the artist directly, misinterpreted lyrics, or overlooked something that changes the classification—we want to hear it. We review every report and will update the reading if the evidence supports it. Abuse of this form will result in a permanent ban.
