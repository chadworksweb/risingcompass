The Tenets

The living constitution of The Rising Compass.

Every song read by the Compass is weighed against these tenets. They are publicly held, publicly visible, and publicly amendable — not by vote, but by deliberation. The burden of changing them is deliberate and high. The words on this page are what we collectively agree on as our moral grounding.
