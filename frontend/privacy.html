Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

The Rising Compass ("we," "us," "our") operates risingcompass.net. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

Automatically collected:

  • Analytics data. We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and PostHog to collect usage data such as pages visited, device type, browser, general geographic region, and interactions with the site. This may include session replays (recordings of your on-page activity, with form fields and sensitive text masked) to help us improve the product. These tools may set cookies on your device. PostHog is served first-party from our own domain. Analytics is anonymous for signed-out visitors; if you sign in to an account, we associate your usage with that account. PostHog stores this data in the United States.
  • IP address. Your IP address may be logged when you submit a Misread Report. It is used solely for abuse prevention.

Voluntarily provided:

  • Misread Reports. If you submit a Misread Report, we collect your first name, last name, email address, and your message. This information is used to review your submission and send a confirmation receipt.
  • Lyrical Charger. If you use the Lyrical Charger feature, we collect the song title and artist you provide. Lyrics are never stored. Only the calibration output — tier, charge value, contamination status, charge summary, and confidence score — is permanently retained in our database. This calibration data becomes part of The Rising Compass lyrical charge database. Your IP address is stored alongside each submission for rate limiting and abuse prevention.

2. Local Storage

The Misread Report form generates a random device identifier stored in your browser's local storage. This is used to prevent abuse and is not linked to your personal identity. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and improve the site
  • To review and respond to Misread Reports
  • To send confirmation emails for submitted reports
  • To prevent abuse and enforce submission limits
  • To understand aggregate traffic patterns via analytics

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics 4 — usage analytics. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • PostHog — product analytics (pageviews and interaction events), with data stored in the United States. See PostHog's Privacy Policy.
  • Resend — email delivery for Misread Report confirmations. See Resend's Privacy Policy.
  • Musixmatch — song search and lyrics retrieval for the Lyrical Charger "Search by Song" feature. Your search queries are sent to Musixmatch to find matching songs. See Musixmatch's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with any other third parties.

5. Data Retention

  • Misread Reports are retained indefinitely for calibration purposes.
  • Lyrical Charger calibrations (tier, charge value, summary) are retained indefinitely as part of The Rising Compass lyrical charge database. Lyrics are never stored.
  • Analytics data is retained per Google's standard GA4 retention policies and PostHog's data retention settings.

6. Your Choices

  • Cookie consent. When you first visit, a cookie bar lets you allow or decline analytics cookies. Visitors in the EU, UK, EEA, and California are opt-in (analytics stay off until you choose "Accept"); elsewhere analytics are on by default and you can decline at any time. You can change your choice whenever you like via the Cookie preferences link (also in the site footer). Essential cookies (sign-in, bot protection, and your saved cookie choice) are always on.
  • We honor your browser's Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals: with either enabled, no analytics data is collected, regardless of any saved cookie choice. You can also block Google Analytics with a browser extension such as Google Analytics Opt-out.
  • You can clear local storage through your browser settings.
  • You are not required to submit any personal information to use the site. The compass readings and historical data are fully accessible without providing any data.

7. California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA) gives you specific rights over your personal information. The categories we may collect are described in Section 1: identifiers (IP address on submissions), internet activity (analytics and interaction data), and any information you voluntarily provide in a Misread Report. We use it only to operate the site, respond to your submissions, and prevent abuse.

  • Right to know and access. You can ask what personal information we have collected about you and how we use it.
  • Right to delete and correct. You can ask us to delete or correct personal information we hold about you, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. There is nothing to opt out of, but we still honor your browser's Global Privacy Control signal as a valid opt-out, and with it enabled no analytics data is collected.
  • No retaliation. We will not deny you service or offer you different terms for exercising any of these rights.

To make a request, contact us through the Misread Report form.

8. Children's Privacy

The Rising Compass is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us through the Misread Report form.
