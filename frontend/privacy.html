The Rising Compass

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 30, 2026

The Rising Compass ("we," "us," "our") operates risingcompass.net. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

Automatically collected:

  • Analytics data. We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to collect anonymous usage data such as pages visited, device type, browser, and general geographic region. GA4 may set cookies on your device. No personally identifiable information is collected through analytics.
  • IP address. Your IP address may be logged when you submit a Misread Report. It is used solely for abuse prevention.

Voluntarily provided:

  • Misread Reports. If you submit a Misread Report, we collect your first name, last name, email address, and your message. This information is used to review your submission and send a confirmation receipt.
  • Lyrical Charger. If you use the Lyrical Charger feature, we collect the song title and artist you provide. Lyrics are never stored. Only the calibration output — tier, charge value, contamination status, charge summary, and confidence score — is permanently retained in our database. This calibration data becomes part of The Rising Compass lyrical charge database. Your IP address is stored alongside each submission for rate limiting and abuse prevention.

2. Local Storage

The Misread Report form generates a random device identifier stored in your browser's local storage. This is used to prevent abuse and is not linked to your personal identity. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and improve the site
  • To review and respond to Misread Reports
  • To send confirmation emails for submitted reports
  • To prevent abuse and enforce submission limits
  • To understand aggregate traffic patterns via analytics

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics 4 — usage analytics. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Resend — email delivery for Misread Report confirmations. See Resend's Privacy Policy.
  • Musixmatch — song search and lyrics retrieval for the Lyrical Charger "Search by Song" feature. Your search queries are sent to Musixmatch to find matching songs. See Musixmatch's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with any other third parties.

5. Data Retention

  • Misread Reports are retained indefinitely for calibration purposes.
  • Lyrical Charger calibrations (tier, charge value, summary) are retained indefinitely as part of The Rising Compass lyrical charge database. Lyrics are never stored.
  • Analytics data is retained per Google's standard GA4 retention policies.

6. Your Choices

  • You can block Google Analytics by using a browser extension such as Google Analytics Opt-out.
  • You can clear local storage through your browser settings.
  • You are not required to submit any personal information to use the site. The compass readings and historical data are fully accessible without providing any data.

7. Children's Privacy

The Rising Compass is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us through the Misread Report form.
