How to verify a prose value

  1. Recompute the hash from the published fields + the revealed prose:
    sha256( "<table>:<id>\n<generated_at>\n<model>\n<prose>" )
  2. Confirm it matches the sha256 in societal-prose.jsonl in the public repo.
  3. Run ots verify batches/<batch>.jsonl.ots -- it proves that batch existed no later than its Bitcoin block. Bitcoin, not the DB or this app, is the trust root.
